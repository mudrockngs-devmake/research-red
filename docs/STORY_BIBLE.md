# Research Red — Story Bible (demo draft)

The animatic is the detailed script; this file holds the rules of the world. Scene specs in `docs/scenes/` override both.

## Premise and tone
Warm, slapstick, kid-friendly early-anime Kanto about grown-ups who never stopped loving Pokemon. You are an adult professor (Maple, Cedar or Sumac; male or female), one of Prof. Oak's two best former students (Elm is the other), visiting Pallet with your wife Ivy for a research stay.

## Story rules
- The Johto starters came to Pallet for a research stay; Ash and Gary's journeys inspire Elm to turn it into a Gym challenge.
- Timeline: Research Red happens alongside the anime, at no stated point in it. Ash and Gary are off on their own journeys and never appear; the anime and movies are homages and background (see docs/reference/, #66).
- The bet: first team to eight badges; losers do Mimey's dishes. Made official by swapping Cell Phone numbers at Daisy's.
- Starter chain: player picks a Kanto starter; Ivy takes the Kanto counter (travels in the player's party); Elm takes the Johto counter; Jenny counters Elm.
- Battles: trainers fight in doubles; you and Ivy are one team, as are Elm and Jenny. Elm and Jenny battle you in or near every Gym city, with lines every two faints and before their last Pokemon.
- Regular trainers: at most one short you-and-Ivy exchange afterward, flavored by trainer class.
- Kukui and Burnett were meant to come but are "starting a league".
- Recurring: a group video call after every badge (Oak, Elm, Jenny, Ivy, player; sometimes Delia or Daisy).
- Endgame payoff: the forms Oak hinted at (Mega Evolution and regional forms; no Z-Moves/Dynamax/Tera this game, #1) become usable late, via a custom area.

## Story rules for towns (owner, 2026-10-19)
- **Town cutscenes and rematches happen just outside Pokémon Centers**: easy to find, a natural place for the cast to meet, and outside because Nurse Joy doesn't allow battles indoors.
- **Elm and Jenny travel along as friends** after Pewter: they don't rush off after scenes. The player and Ivy still do their own thing, but the group of four can meet up in towns (watching a Gym, visiting the Museum...).
- **Oak's lab after the story:** Oak is a rematch that grows with every badge (EXP, no money); Daisy drops by for it with Muffin. Oak no longer asks about the Pokédex (that's the PC's job).

## The League's interest (owner, 2026-10-19)
The Pokémon League wants to learn more about the research. That's why extras arrive with the grant (mysteriously, from the League: e.g. held items that never break), why gym leaders accept rematches anytime, and why they bring bigger, more varied teams: to help the player bring out the starters' potential.

## Running gags
Gerald the Tentacool on the anchor line; Slowpoke never hung up the call; "Persian" Muffin; the lab carpet; Elm's "warm-up"; Jenny's reports with diagrams; Mimey's dishes.

## The research payoff (owner, 2026-10-19)
- Why this team works: the player and Ivy study regional forms (Ivy in the field, the player on the molecular side: sequencing and analysis), Elm is the evolution expert (and Jenny's along for fun). Alternate forms of the starters sit right where their fields meet.
- The research goal is the starters' alternate forms. Its payoff, after the Elite Four, is **Mega Evolution and regional forms**: those systems fit the story; no system goes in just for the sake of it. When and where is still open (a large new area or a reworked Sevii Islands could host it).
- **Z-Moves, Dynamax / Gigantamax and Tera stay out of this game** (issue #1), saved for a possible sequel.

## Sequel seeds (spitballing, nothing decided)
A Johto game where Elm is the lead researcher (as Oak is here). The player and Ivy face Professors Kukui and Burnet as a couple of rivals in the Johto challenge. Their research goal: Z-Moves (Kukui's specialty), Ultra Wormholes (Burnet's), possibly leading to Eternatus and Gigantamax. Mentions in the Daisy scene lay groundwork for it.
