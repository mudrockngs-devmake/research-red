# Reference: the anime and Gold/Silver/Crystal, area by area

A guide for building wild Pokémon tables (`data/research_red/encounters/`) and
ambient Pokémon (#64): what each area had in the anime and in Generation II, so
tables can carry homages. Not game data; nothing here is used by the build.

Summarised from Bulbapedia (episode and location pages, read 2026-10-20) and
PokémonDB (Generation II location tables). Gen II tables give rarity labels, not
percentages. Add an area here when the game reaches it.

Key: **EP001** = episode code. "Origins" = *Pokémon Origins* (2013 special),
"Generations" = *Pokémon Generations* (2016 shorts).

## Pallet Town
**Anime**
- EP001 *Pokémon - I Choose You!*: the TV battle Ash watches is **Gengar vs. Nidorino**, then **Onix**. Ash's alarm clock is a **Voltorb**. The three starters are already taken, so Ash gets **Pikachu**. Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle appear in his dream.
- Professor Oak's lab has the **Oak Corral**, a big reserve split by type (rocky areas, desert, grass, lakes) where Trainers' extra Pokémon live. Ash's Bulbasaur keeps the peace there.
- The **Xanadu Nursery** near town is a greenhouse run by Florinda Showers and her **Gloom** (*Make Room for Gloom*). Pallet also has a radio station, WPOK, run by Masae from her general store.
- Origins File 1: Red picks Charmander and Blue picks Squirtle.
- Evolutions *The Discovery*: Elaine's Pikachu picks her a bouquet in Pallet.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal water** (all high level; postgame Kanto)
- Surf: Tentacool (common), Tentacruel (uncommon)
- Old Rod: Magikarp, Tentacool. Good Rod: Tentacool, Magikarp, Shellder, Chinchou. Super Rod: Shellder, Chinchou, Tentacruel, Lanturn

**Used in Research Red:** Gengar vs. Nidorino on the TV at home. Tentacool, Magikarp, Shellder and Chinchou in the sea. Gyarados as an ambient Pokémon (#64). Ideas: an Oak Corral feel for the Pager Pokémon, a Gloom near a garden, a Voltorb clock in the bedroom.

## Route 1
**Anime** (EP001)
- **Pidgey**: Ash's first catch attempt; it escapes and uses Gust and Sand Attack.
- **Rattata** rummages through Ash's bag for food.
- Ash throws a rock at what he thinks is a Pidgey. It's a **Spearow**, which calls its **flock** to chase him and Pikachu to a river.
- Ash nearly becomes lunch for a **Gyarados** in the river, with **Magikarp** there too.
- **Ho-Oh** flies over the rainbow at the end.
- Also debuting in EP001: **Sandshrew, Mankey, Dodrio**.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal grass**
- Morning/day: Pidgey (common), Rattata (common), Sentret (uncommon), Furret (rare, Lv 6)
- Night: Rattata (common), Hoothoot (common), Raticate (rare, Lv 6)

**Used:** the Spearow flock (joint most common by day), Pidgey, Rattata, Pikachu, Mankey, Doduo (Dodrio's first form). Sentret and Hoothoot as in Gen II. Ideas: Ho-Oh over a rainbow as a story moment, and a Dodrio crowing as an ambient Pokémon.

## Viridian City
**Anime**
- EP002 *Pokémon Emergency!*: Officer Jenny drives Ash to the Pokémon Center, where Nurse Joy's **Chansey** carry Pikachu and the Center's **Pikachu** charge him up. Team Rocket (Jessie's **Ekans**, James's **Koffing**, **Meowth**) attack, and Misty's **Goldeen** flops on dry land. Misty was fishing when Ash borrowed her bike.
- *The Misty Mermaid*: Misty's **Horsea** looks unwell swimming in a Viridian fountain.
- *The Battle of the Badge*: a **Fearow** carries off Togepi. Gary battles Giovanni (with Mewtwo) at the Viridian Gym.
- *Gotta Catch Ya Later!* (end of Gold and Silver): back in Viridian. The original Nurse Joy has retired, and Misty's bike has finally been fixed.
- *The Scheme Team* (after Hoenn): a friendly battle with Agatha at the rebuilt Gym; Ash meets Scott of the Battle Frontier.
- Origins File 1: Red heals Charmander here and calls Oak. File 3: Red battles Giovanni (Rhyhorn, Rhydon).

**Gold/Silver/Crystal water**
- Surf: Poliwag (common), Poliwhirl (uncommon)
- Old Rod: Magikarp, Poliwag. Good Rod: Poliwag, Magikarp. Super Rod: Poliwag, Poliwhirl, Magikarp

**Used:** Psyduck and Goldeen common (Misty), Poliwag second. Ideas: Horsea in a fountain as an ambient Pokémon; Chansey or the Center's Pikachu inside; Misty's fixed bike as a joke.

## Route 22
**Anime:** no notable episode found. In the games it's the road to the League gate and the first rival battle.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal**
- Grass, morning/day: Rattata, Spearow (common), Doduo (uncommon), Ponyta and Fearow (rare)
- Grass, night: Rattata, Poliwag (common), Ponyta (rare)
- Surf: Poliwag (common), Poliwhirl (uncommon)
- Rods: Magikarp and Poliwag (Old, Good); Poliwag, Poliwhirl, Magikarp (Super)

**Used:** Rattata, Spearow, Doduo and Ponyta as in Gen II; Poliwag is the river's star.

## Route 2
**Anime:** only a brief look at the north end in EP005, and in Origins File 1.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal grass**
- Morning/day: Caterpie, Weedle, Pidgey (common); Ledyba (morning); Metapod, Kakuna, Butterfree, Beedrill (uncommon); Pidgeotto, Pikachu, Ledian (rare)
- Night: Hoothoot, Spinarak (common), Noctowl (uncommon), Ariados (rare)

**Used:** Caterpie, Weedle, Pidgey, Ledyba, Hoothoot and Spinarak as in Gen II. The evolved ones are left out (our evolution-level rule).

## Viridian Forest
Not in Gold/Silver/Crystal (the forest was cut down to a small clearing).

**Anime**
- EP002–EP003: Ash catches **Caterpie** (his first catch), then **Pidgeotto**. Caterpie evolves into **Metapod**. Misty is scared of bugs; a **Beedrill** appears.
- EP004 *Challenge of the Samurai*: Ash fails to catch a **Weedle**. Samurai's **Pinsir** and **Metapod** fight Ash's: the Metapod Harden standoff. A **Beedrill** swarm takes Ash's Metapod, **Kakuna** evolve into Beedrill, and Metapod evolves into **Butterfree** to save Ash.
- *Pallet Party Panic*: a **Spearow** flock led by a **Fearow** (said to be the Spearow Ash hit with a rock in EP001) fights the forest's **Pidgey** and **Pidgeotto**. Ash's Pidgeotto evolves into **Pidgeot** and stays to guard them.
- Journeys *The Rainbow and the Pokémon Master!*: Pidgeot returns to help Ash in the forest.
- Generations *The Adventure*: Red catches a **Pikachu** here, then battles a **Caterpie** as **Zapdos** flies over.

**Used:** Caterpie, Weedle, Metapod, Kakuna, Pikachu; Butterfree and Beedrill at Lv 10; Pinsir rare (Samurai). Ideas: a Samurai homage trainer with Pinsir and Metapod, a sleeping Beedrill hive as ambient Pokémon, a Pidgeot as the forest's guardian (ambient), and a Zapdos fly-over.

## Pewter City
**Anime**
- EP005 *Showdown in Pewter City*: Brock's **Onix** and **Geodude**. Flint (Brock's father) charges Pikachu at an old hydroelectric plant, and the Gym sprinklers help beat Onix. Brock's nine younger siblings live here.
- *A Family That Battles Together Stays Together!* (Chronicles): Brock's mother Lola turns the Gym into a Water-type Gym for a while.
- DP: Forrest becomes the Gym Leader.
- Journeys *Restore and Renew!*: at the Pewter Museum, Goh restores an Old Amber into **Aerodactyl**.
- Origins File 1: Red visits the Museum, then beats Brock.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal:** no wild Pokémon.

**Used:** Elm cries at a fossil in the Museum (rematch scene). Ideas: Brock's siblings as townsfolk, a Museum amber/Aerodactyl nod (#49).
