# How the cast talks: the early anime, and our take

A companion to [STYLE_GUIDE.md](../STYLE_GUIDE.md) (which rules). It covers every
character we have a sprite or portrait for, as they were in the **early anime**
(Kanto, Orange Islands, Johto; roughly 1997–2002), with its slapstick comedy.
Later series changed several of them a lot; we leave those versions out.

Our versions are sitcom grown-ups (two married couples, friends and family), so
the anime is seasoning, not a script: borrow the habits, keep our relationships.

Sources: Bulbapedia character pages (read 2026-10-20 via the Internet Archive).
Lines marked *(memory)* are from Claude's memory of the episodes, not from the
pages; check them before quoting an episode.

## Professor Oak
**Early anime:** Pallet Town's Pokémon professor, a few houses up the road from
Delia, and a close friend of hers. Famously forgetful, given to antics, and
writes **senryū** (haiku-style poems about Pokémon) at odd moments. His research
is how people and Pokémon live together. In the novels he became a professor at
Celadon University at 25. Grandfather of Gary. He's dramatic about small
things and gets distracted mid-sentence by a Pokémon. *(memory: his poem-reading
segments at the end of early episodes; long pauses before remembering a name.)*

**Speech habits to borrow:** an "Ah!" or "Ho ho!" before a revelation; trailing
off when distracted; a three-line poem dropped into serious moments; warm,
grandfatherly pride.

**Our take:** the grant-giving mentor who adores all of them, half-remembers
everyone's names, and turns research supplies into ceremonies.
- *Oak: Behold! The Ranch Pager! Er… it's in your PC. Behold that, later.*

## Ivy (the player's wife)
**Early anime:** Professor Philena Ivy of **Valencia Island** (Orange Islands),
expert in **regional differences in Pokémon** (her paper on "adaptive variation
as a function of regional distribution" ran in *Pokémon Researchers Monthly*).
Confident, brilliant, a good friend of Oak's, and the one who sent the GS Ball
plot rolling (*Poké Ball Peril*). She runs her lab with three assistants
*(memory: Faith, Hope and Charity)*. Brock stayed on as her assistant and came
back heartbroken: the only crush that ever "traumatized" him. In *The Power of
One* she's in the helicopter with Oak and Delia.

**Speech habits to borrow:** brisk and certain about science; fun facts about how
a Pokémon changed by region; cool composure while chaos happens around her.

**Our take:** warm and sharp, the real expert in regional forms, calls the
player "sweetie", and gets the best quiet jokes. Brock's broken heart can be a
running joke she pretends not to understand.
- *Ivy: Raticate here are bigger than on Valencia. Island Rattata are tiny. And rude.*

## Elm
**Early anime (Johto):** sweet and kind-hearted, enormously knowledgeable, and
prone to **heated arguments over theories** and **long-winded explanations**
when anyone asks a simple question. **Absent-minded**: wrapped up in his work,
he forgets his surroundings and can stay in his lab for days. *(memory: flustered
and clumsy around visitors; studies Pokémon Eggs and evolution.)*

**Speech habits to borrow:** answers a yes/no question with a lecture; "Now,
the fascinating thing is…"; loses track of what he was holding; stubborn in
a debate, then generous when he loses.

**Our take:** loud, competitive, secretly sentimental, discovered Eggs,
dessert names for his Pokémon. Jenny cuts his lectures short.
- *Elm: Simple answer? Of course! It begins, as all great things do, with an Egg…*
- *Jenny: Dear. Yes or no.*

## Jenny (Elm's wife) and Officer Jenny (her cousin)
**Early anime:** there are **many Officer Jennys**, one per town, who all look
the same. Only Brock can tell them apart (by small differences, and the badge on
the hat). They're brave and determined but sometimes ineffective, and usually
have a **Growlithe** as a police dog. *(memory: the Viridian Jenny's motorbike;
"Halt!" at suspicious-looking travelers, like Ash carrying Pikachu; announcing
alerts by loudspeaker.)*

**Speech habits to borrow:** brisk orders ("Halt!", "Stand back!"); suspicion
first, apologies after; protocol and procedure; family pride in the Jenny line.

**Our take:** Jenny is off duty, deadpan and loving: "in my professional
opinion", "by protocol", calls Elm "dear". Officer Jenny is on duty, brisk, and
in on the "every Jenny looks the same" family jokes.
- *Officer Jenny: Halt! …Oh. Cousin. Halt anyway, it's procedure.*

## Delia
**Early anime:** reserved, quiet and cheerful, and formidable when angry (she
told off an Entei in *Spell of the Unown*). Gardener (*Make Room for Gloom*:
three to four hundred pounds of fertilizer from the Xanadu Nursery), a great cook
(she took over a restaurant's kitchen at the Indigo Plateau in *A Friend in
Deed*), and persevering. Lives with Mimey. *(memory: her famous send-off line
about changing your underwear every day; fussing over packed supplies.)*

**Speech habits to borrow:** gentle and chatty, practical reminders, food as
love, sudden steel when someone misbehaves.

**Our take:** motherly, feeds everyone, pancakes, heals your team or lets you
rest the night (#56).
- *Delia: Did you pack snacks? Rubber gloves? Clean socks? Every day, mind you.*

## Daisy
**Games:** Gary's older sister. Pokémon adore her and calm down around her;
she grooms Pokémon, has **tea every day** (bought at the Celadon Department
Store), and was **Grand Champion of the Spring Pokémon Contest** (FireRed's
Fame Checker). The early anime barely shows her *(memory: she appears in
*Pokémon Chronicles*)*.

**Speech habits to borrow:** calm and practical, tea in hand, gently unimpressed.

**Our take:** unimpressed by Elm, corrects "Persian" to "Rattata", Muffin's owner.
- *Daisy: Muffin is a Rattata. She's also a contest champion's Rattata. Sit, please.*

## Mimey (Delia's Mr. Mime)
**Early anime:** at first it only listens to Delia. It **loves doing the
housework** and doesn't like sharing the job; it got competitive with Brock over
chores. It protects Delia's garden (Light Screen against Team Rocket), cleans and
vacuums by pantomime, and once vacuumed Ash awake. Later it's the household's
voice of reason.

**Speech habits:** "Mime! Mime!" only; jokes are physical (bows, invisible walls,
mimed dishes).

**Our take:** makes pancakes, and the bet's prize is doing its dishes.
- *Mimey: Mime mime! (It mimes washing a very large, very invisible stack of dishes.)*

## Muffin (Daisy's Rattata) and Slowpoke (the lab's)
Ours, with anime-style Pokémon comedy: they only say their names, and the joke is
in timing.
- **Muffin:** "Rat! Tat tat!", extremely pleased with herself; often mistaken for
  a Persian by Elm.
- **Slowpoke:** "…Slow?", always one beat late, reacting to the line before last.
