# Pewter: watching Elm and Jenny take on Brock (#69)

Status: first draft approved by the owner (2026-10-20); finalise in the v0.0.16
dialogue pass. A scripted fight on the battle screen since v0.0.23 (#142, owner 2026-10-30:
proper in-battle cutscene instead of overworld sprites, not too long, funny; the lines stay,
Elm and Jenny keep their starters out and sweep two per turn).

## Flow
1. You beat Brock (Elm and Jenny saw the end from the Gym doorway).
2. Rematch with Elm and Jenny outside the Pokémon Center, whenever you like.
3. Elm: "We're challenging Brock next. Want to come watch?" Yes: fade into the
   Gym, you and Ivy on the side bench. No: they go ahead; walk into the Gym later
   and the scene plays then.
4. Brock sends two at a time against the pair, in three rounds:
   - **Geodude and Sandshrew:** the warm-up. Elm lectures Brock ("Did you know
     Onix used to evolve only by trading? Now you just—" / "…Are you lecturing me
     in my own Gym?" / Jenny: "He lectures everyone. It's how he says hello.").
     Ivy and the player bet on how soon he mentions Eggs ("Ten seconds." /
     "Five." … "Four."). A super-effective hit; Jenny: "The evidence suggests Rock
     types don't care for that." / Elm: "That was MY observation!" / "Case closed, dear."
   - **Kabuto and Omanyte:** Elm loses it ("LIVING FOSSILS! In a GYM!"), echoing
     his tears at the Museum; Jenny: "Dear. Command." (twice). Ivy and the player
     bet on whether he'll ask Brock where he got them; he does, mid-battle.
   - **Rhyhorn and Onix:** "It's so much… bigger in person." / "Dear. Command." /
     "Right! For science!" Onix binds Cruller, who doesn't budge; the player:
     "(Cruller has the composure of someone who's done a lot of paperwork.)"
5. Brock: "Fair and square. The Boulder Badge is yours." Elm: "It's allergies!
   This Gym is very… rocky!" Brock to Ivy: "Professor Ivy, your notes on regional
   forms helped my breeding more than I can say." / "Then they did their job.
   Thank you, Brock."
6. Fade to the four outside the Gym door. Elm: "One badge each! The race is ON!"
   / Jenny: "And dinner at Delia's is still on the line, dear." / Player: "I've
   already started planning the menu. For you to cook." / Ivy: "Sweetie."

Elm's and Jenny's Pokémon are shown by their real names (their lab starters by
the player's pick, Cruller, and the rest of their Pewter team).
