# Professor Oak

Character sheet: who he is, how he talks, what he'd never do. Workshopped with
the owner on 2026-10-20 (the first sheet; the template for the rest of the cast).
Voice reference from the early anime: [VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
A grandfather to everyone: he raised the player and Elm, his two best students,
like his own children, and now quietly makes sure his grown-up kids and their
wives have everything they need.

## Three words
Warm, theatrical, forgetful (about small things).

## Relationships
| Who | How Oak sees them |
|---|---|
| **The player** | One of his two best students. Proud of the person they became and of the life they built with Ivy. |
| **Elm** | His other best student, every bit as capable. Loves his passion, the same grandfatherly love in a different key. Enjoys the rivalry and quietly fuels it. |
| **Ivy** | A colleague he respects greatly (her Orange Islands work on regional forms). Their banter is a running pleasure for both. |
| **Jenny** (Elm's wife) | Genuinely wants to know her, because she matters to his student. She is not "a Jenny" to him; she is Elm's Jenny. |
| **Delia** | Old friend up the road. Tea, chats and gossip about the kids. Proud of Ash together (the anime's "So am I, Delia. So am I."). |
| **Daisy** | His granddaughter. Teams up with him for the rematches, with Muffin. |
| **Ash and Gary** | He cares about both openly and speaks of them warmly. No coyness. |

## How he talks
- Talks *to* people, always. Even tutorial and system-style information comes as Oak talking to the player, Elm, Ivy or Jenny, never as the game reading out rules.
- Theatrical about joy ("Behold!", "Ho ho!"), never about scolding.
- Forgets small things (a name for a beat, where he put the Pager), never the important ones.
- Poems at odd moments.
- Pokémon twists on sayings: "The early Pidgey catches the Weedle." "Don't count your Exeggcute before they hatch."
- Old lecturer habits (he taught at Celadon University): asides, grading things, "As I used to tell my class…".

## What he does in the story
- Funded the research stay with a League grant and arranged everything quietly: called the Gym Leaders ahead (told them not to go easy, secretly asked them to keep the kids safe).
- Hands out the starters and the grant supplies, including the **Ranch Pager** (#29) so his Pokémon can keep them safe on the road; the items wait in the player's PC (Start > PC works anywhere).
- His Pokémon do the field moves: Farfetch'd, Pikachu, Abra, Machop, Tauros, Pidgeot, Lapras, Gyarados, Diglett.
- Hints at "forms in other lands and other eras": Mega Evolution and regional forms after the Elite Four (#1).
- On the group video call after every badge.
- **Rematch (#53):** unlocks after the 6v6 with Elm and Jenny on Route 22, then scales with badges. A double with Daisy: Oak's AI runs both sides' Pokémon, Daisy brings her sprite, her lines and Muffin. Gives EXP, not money.
- **Muffin stays a Rattata forever** (a running joke): Daisy loves her as she is and Muffin doesn't want to evolve, so she just keeps getting stronger.

## He would never
- Be cruel or shout in anger.
- Be truly scatterbrained about what matters (his students, his Pokémon, their safety).
- Sound like a rules screen.

## Jokes he's in
Slapstick and warm: the forgetful beat that lands on something heartfelt, a
poem in the wrong moment, tall-grass warnings nobody listens to, the lab's
cables and aides, Pokémon-flavoured proverbs.
