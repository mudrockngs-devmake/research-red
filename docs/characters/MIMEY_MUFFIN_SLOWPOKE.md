# Mimey, Muffin and Slowpoke

Character notes, 2026-10-20. Pokémon characters only say their names; the
comedy is physical and in the timing.

## Mimey (Delia's Mr. Mime)
- Makes pancakes, does the dishes, and is **proud of its housework**: it doesn't like sharing the job (the anime's Mimey got competitive with Brock over chores). Protects Delia's garden; cleans by pantomime.
- Lines: "Mime! Mime mime!" plus mimed actions (bows, invisible walls, very large invisible stacks of dishes).
- The bet: when the losers cook dinner at Delia's, Mimey is outraged someone else is in its kitchen and supervises every step.
- Delia's lead Pokémon in her optional battle (#70), under its nickname. Part of the travelling Pallet party later.
- The player is genuinely fond of it; Ivy thinks it's adorable that Delia has a friend like Mimey.

## Muffin (Daisy's Rattata)
- "Rat! Tat tat!", extremely pleased with herself: a ribbon winner from Daisy's contest days.
- **Rattata forever**: doesn't want to evolve; in the rematch she holds an Eviolite and just gets tougher.
- Elm's nemesis ("the eyes of a Persian"), slowly becoming his friend with Daisy's coaching. Ivy finds her fascinating (Alolan Rattata!).

## Slowpoke (the lab's)
- "…Slow?", always one beat late, reacting to the line before last.
- Never hung up the intro call.
- In Oak's rematch team; at some later badge it turns up as a **Galarian Slowking** and nobody can explain how (#53). Every badge call can have a new theory.
