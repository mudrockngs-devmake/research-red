# Daisy

Character sheet, drafted by Claude on 2026-10-20 at the owner's request (the
owner will learn her and adjust). Voice reference:
[VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
Oak's calm, kind granddaughter: a former Contest Grand Champion whom every
Pokémon in Pallet trusts, who quietly keeps her grandfather's house (and
schedule) running and can't be rattled, not even by Elm.

## Three words
Calm, kind, unflappable.

## Her own thing
- **Former Grand Champion of the Spring Pokémon Contest** (from FireRed). Her Rattata **Muffin** won ribbons too, which is why Muffin is so pleased with herself.
- **A gifted groomer:** Pokémon calm down around her. Her dream is a little grooming salon in Pallet.
- **Tea every day,** bought specially at the Celadon Department Store.
- **The bet's referee:** she lives with Oak and hosts Elm and Jenny, so she's the neutral one who keeps the official badge scoreboard.

## Relationships
| Who | How Daisy sees them |
|---|---|
| **Oak** | Looks after him (tea, schedule, finding his glasses); fondly translates his forgetfulness; his rematch partner (#53). |
| **Gary** | Her little brother: proud of him, teases him (he took a Town Map; Ash borrowed the other). |
| **Ash** | Like a cousin from down the road. Still waiting for that Town Map. |
| **Elm** | Her houseguest. Endlessly patient with him; coaches him, slowly, into making peace with Muffin ("It's a Rattata, Elm. Her name is Muffin."). |
| **Jenny** | A fellow calm one; they have tea while their houseguest-husband panics about a Rattata. Jenny appreciates her patience with Elm. |
| **Ivy** | Ivy finds Muffin fascinating from a regional-forms angle; Daisy is protective ("Muffin is not a research subject." / "…A little bit of one."). |
| **The player** | Friendly; happy to groom their Pokémon. |
| **Delia** | Neighbour; part of the Pallet tea circle with Oak. |

## How she talks
Short, calm, gently firm one-liners. The comedy is her being unbothered while
chaos happens, and Muffin being the star of every room.
- "Muffin has claimed the chair. Elm will have to stand."
- "Grandpa is going to pretend he's not excited about this."

## She would never
- Get flustered or raise her voice.
- Be unkind to any Pokémon, or let anyone call Muffin a Persian unchallenged.
- Take sides in the bet (she's the referee).
