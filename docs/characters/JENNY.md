# Jenny

Character sheet, workshopped with the owner on 2026-10-20. Voice reference:
[VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
New Bark Town's Officer Jenny, a detective and a hard worker, married to Elm
because his passion is everything her procedure isn't; on this trip she's
finally off duty, with her husband, her friends and a lot of Pokémon.

## Three words (proposed)
Deadpan, devoted, sharp.

## Backstory
- Was New Bark Town's Jenny, a **detective**; that's where she met Elm.
- The player got to know her when Elm and Jenny married. The two of them went on
  Pokémon-spotting drives on her motorbike back then.
- What she wants: a break. She spends all her time on the job, and this research
  trip (work, technically) is the breath of fresh air she needs: time with Elm,
  her friends and family, and an excuse to see Pokémon like on the old spotting drives.

## Relationships
| Who | How Jenny sees them |
|---|---|
| **Elm** | His passion is what drew her to him, and it complements her procedure. She's his rock: regulates without dimming, translates Ivy's jokes for him, advises him against things (walking into tall grass unarmed), reminds him to be a good sport, always on his side. Calls him "dear". |
| **Ivy** | Best friends; their own fun gossip ritual; knowing looks about their husbands. |
| **The player** | Deadpan duo and crisis team; same old Kanto TV shows; the spotting drives; asks how to handle Elm. Rates their jokes. |
| **Oak** | Touched that he sees her, not "a Jenny". |
| **Daisy** | Appreciates how sweet and patient Daisy is with Elm, who can rub people the wrong way. |
| **The Jenny family** | Huge; she runs into cousins across Kanto (cutscenes like Viridian's). Nurse Joys maybe later. |
| **Her Growlithe** | Her police partner, with a pastry name like Elm's Pokémon. Seen with them from Daisy's house on; on her team; Arcanine later. |

## How she talks
- Bone-dry deadpan; her deadpan is love.
- Procedure as comedy ("Sandwiches are labeled. Crusts left, crustless right.").
- Reports with diagrams.
- Detective-show flavour, written with love for *Case Closed* (*Detective Conan*), reworked for Pokémon: the deduction, the reveal (draft set below, being workshopped).
- Anime-Jenny habits: playfully suspicious first, apologetic after ("Halt!"), then reminded she's off duty.

## Catchphrases (draft, workshopping)
At most one per scene, so they stay special.
- **Everyday deduction:** "The evidence suggests…" (setup) then "Case closed." (punchline).
- **Big moments, rare:** "One truth prevails." Or to Elm, softly: "There's only ever one truth, dear."
- **Off-duty slips:** "Halt!" … "Off duty, dear." … "…Halt, recreationally."
- **The relaxing arc:** "In my professional opinion" slowly becomes "In my off-duty opinion" as the trip goes on.

## The relaxing arc
Everyone, Elm most of all, wants Jenny to relax and not be a cop unless she has
to. Elm goes out of his way to help her unwind (badly, sweetly); the player and
Ivy help in funny ways. Her instincts keep clocking in anyway.

## Also
- Connected to the **International Police**, so she can act at that level if a story ever needs it.
- **Brock** (later): funny and professional with her, never advances.
- **Cruller** (her Growlithe): she wanted a proper police name; Elm explained the name in a lecture (with citations) and changed her mind. "His lecture had citations. I lost."
- Written partly as a love letter to *Case Closed* fans.

## She would never
- Be cold or mean.
- Undercut Elm in public, or let him get hurt.
- Lose her cool in a crisis.
