# Delia

Character sheet, workshopped with the owner on 2026-10-20. Voice reference:
[VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
Ash's mom and the player and Ivy's host in Pallet: she treats them like her own
kids, feeds everyone, and hides a competitive streak that makes her a real
Trainer when she wants to be.

## Three words (proposed)
Warm, cheerful, quietly formidable.

## Relationships
| Who | How Delia sees them |
|---|---|
| **The player and Ivy** | Her guests, treated like her own kids. The player is warmly grateful (wholesome only). |
| **Oak** | Old friend up the road; tea and gossip about the kids; proud of Ash together ("So am I, Delia. So am I."). |
| **Mimey** | Housemate and friend; her lead Pokémon in battle, nickname and all. |
| **Ash** | Her son, off on his journey. She knows the people he stayed with, like Professors Kukui and Burnet in Alola. |
| **Elm and Jenny, Daisy** | Neighbours for the stay; the bet's losers cook her dinner and she gets the night off. |

## How she talks
- Cheerful, practical, food as love ("Nobody wins on an empty stomach.").
- Gentle reminders, sudden steel when someone misbehaves (the anime's Delia told off an Entei).
- Hidden competitive streak and ambitions (garden, cooking, battling); Brock once said she'd make a great Trainer.

## Battling Delia
- Available after the 6v6 with Elm and Jenny on Route 22. When you heal at her house, she offers a battle in a short exchange; you can say no and just heal or rest.
- A **double battle**, Delia alone with two Pokémon out, Mimey leading under its nickname.
- Scales with your badges, like Oak's rematch (#53).
- Team ideas (to decide): Mimey; garden and kitchen Pokémon (Bellossom or Gloom from the Xanadu Nursery, Chansey, Miltank); maybe Pokémon Ash left at home, anime-style.

## Later in the game
A travelling Pallet party (Oak, Daisy, Delia and Mimey) turns up in places to
see how things are going: cutscenes and dialogue so they aren't stuck in their
houses. Not in the demo.

## She would never
- Be unkind, or nosy in a mean way.
- Let anyone leave hungry.
