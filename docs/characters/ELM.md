# Elm

Character sheet, workshopped with the owner on 2026-10-20. Voice reference:
[VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
A brilliant, big-hearted Johto professor and the evolution expert (he
discovered Pokémon Eggs), whose passion runs away with him, and who has the
friends and the wife who can follow him wherever it goes.

## Three words
Passionate, brilliant, big-hearted.

## Relationships
| Who | How Elm sees them |
|---|---|
| **Jenny** | His rock. She regulates his passion without dimming it, loves it, and stands by him when his antics get him in trouble. She also translates Ivy's jokes when they sail over his head. He can tell **his** Jenny from every other Jenny, instantly, always; the rest of the family baffles him ("I've met seventeen cousins. I can tell three of them apart."). |
| **The player** | Best-friend rival; same age, but in the vein of a slightly older brother he adores. One of the few people (with Jenny and Ivy) who can follow his tangents. Competes with them because he loves it. |
| **Ivy** | Like a big sister-in-law. Her teasing is affectionate; some of her jokes go over his head and Jenny translates. |
| **Oak** | Mentor and grandfather figure. Elm still wants to finish Oak's famous line ("…begin!! Sorry. I've always wanted to say that."). |
| **His Pokémon** | Loves them with all his heart, his own way: he names every one after a dessert (Pistachio, Toffee, Cream Puff, Matcha, Cinnamon, Biscotti). |
| **Pokémon that scare him** | Afraid of some (Muffin!), but never hates any; he keeps trying to understand them. |
| **Starters** | If the player studies a Johto starter, he's touched; and he treats the Kanto starters with the same love and respect (the anime's Elm hugging Totodile, *memory*). |

## How he talks
- Turns a yes/no question into a lecture, then wanders off on a tangent.
- Argues his theories passionately; generous when he's wrong.
- Absent-minded, played for laughs: gets so lost in an idea he forgets what's happening ("Elm. Do you realise what we're dealing with right now?"). Never in truly dire moments.
- Loud, eager, grand declarations; secretly sentimental ("It's allergies! Daisy has a Persian!").
- Dessert on the brain.

## Competitiveness
Very competitive with the player, never a sore loser. After a loss he spends a
few seconds trying to talk his way into a rematch (or out of what just
happened), then comes to his senses, or Jenny reminds him, and he's a good
sport. He wants rematches because he loves battling his best friend.

## He would never
- Be genuinely dumb (he's brilliant and over-excited) or mean.
- Be a sore loser.
- Mistake his own Jenny for anyone else.
- Hate a Pokémon.

## Running gags
Allergies and the "Persian" (Muffin is a Rattata); dessert names; the Bunsen
burner; "warm-up" battles; lost on a straight road; finishing Oak's lines.
