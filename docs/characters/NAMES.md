# What the couples call each other

Decided with the owner on 2026-10-20. Each person addresses their partner their
own way, so the two couples never sound the same (the text boxes have no voices,
so these habits help tell the speakers apart).

| Who | Calls their partner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ivy** → the player | "sweetie" | Warm and teasing. |
| **The player** → Ivy | "Ivy" | Dry, plain. A rare pet name is saved for sincere moments (to be picked later). |
| **Jenny** → Elm | "dear" | Calm and steady. |
| **Elm** → Jenny | "Jenny!", sometimes "my Jenny" | Loud and excited; ties into his gag that he always knows *his* Jenny. |

The player can be male or female; either way they're married to Ivy. Lines
about the player never assume a gender ("spouse", "partner" or their name).
