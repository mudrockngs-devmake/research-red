# Ivy

Character sheet, workshopped with the owner on 2026-10-20. Voice reference:
[VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md](../reference/VOICES_EARLY_ANIME.md).

## In one line
The player's wife: a confident, strong woman and the foremost expert on regional
forms, who teases the player lovingly and adores their jokes.

## Three words
Cute, strong, confident.

## Relationships
| Who | How Ivy sees them |
|---|---|
| **The player** | Her spouse and research partner. She teases them with love, laughs at their jokes, and worries quietly when they're not OK. Calls them "sweetie". |
| **Elm** | Teases him like a little brother (he's like the player's little brother too). Respects his work completely. |
| **Jenny** | Her best friend, for her own reasons. The two wives share knowing looks at their husbands' shenanigans and play off each other to get through the weird moments: sitcom energy, never a literal fourth-wall break. They have their own gossip style, fun and funny, like Oak and Delia's. |
| **The four of them** | Two couples who go through anything, thick or thin, each pair with its own dynamic. |
| **Oak** | A colleague with mutual respect (she didn't study under him). He's thrilled she and his student found each other. |
| **Delia** | Their warm host for the research stay. |
| **Daisy** | Her respected colleague's granddaughter; friendly. |
| **Ash and Gary** | Familiar with both. |
| **Mimey** | Thinks it's adorable that Delia has a friend like Mimey. |
| **Muffin** | Loves her, and as a regional-forms expert finds a Rattata endlessly interesting (Alolan Rattata!). |
| **Brock** (later) | Purely professional and friendly. He learned about regional Pokémon outside Kanto from her for his breeding work. No romance, no awkwardness, no competition. |

## How she talks
- Sitcom levels of corny, never cringey.
- Warm, quick, a little teasing; "sweetie" for the player, "dear" back at Jenny's habit.
- Chaos doesn't rattle her: that's just life with her work (and her husband).
- Fun facts about how Pokémon change by region, often romantic about science ("Regional forms can start with one stubborn ancestor. Science is so romantic.").
- Gets her own jokes; never only the straight man.

## Story jobs
Takes the Kanto counter-starter and travels in the player's party; doubles
partner; walks behind as the follower; chats while exploring; comments on new
Pokédex entries; shares forms wisdom learned from other professors (Kukui,
Burnet).

## She would never
- Tease to hurt, or tease anyone who's actually down.
- Be jealous or competitive about the player.
- Look down on anyone's research, Elm's included.
- Be helpless, or need rescuing to move the plot.
- Sound like a rulebook.
- Gossip meanly. Her gossip with Jenny is its own fun, kind ritual (like Oak and Delia's tea chats). The anime's Brock story stays out.
